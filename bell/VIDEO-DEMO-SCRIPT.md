# Bell demo video

Target length: 75–90 seconds. The video is a product demonstration, not a
promise that the public browser can make arbitrary authenticated CMC calls.
Use the live integrity monitor and show the freshness badge and receipt link.

## Story

The investor has found several tokens under one stock, ETF or commodity. The
question is not yet which wrapper is best. The first question is whether the
wrappers deserve to be compared at all.

## Shot list

| Time | Screen / wireframe | Voice and on-screen message |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Dark hero, Bell mark, `Before you compare, check the wrapper` | “CoinMarketCap can show you the candidates. Bell checks whether the grouping is safe to compare.” |
| 5–14s | Hero search with `Silver` entered | “Start with the asset you are researching. No account and no trading signal.” |
| 14–28s | Silver result: `5 representations · 4 issuers · 31.05× observed price spread` | “Silver has five representations from four issuers. The observed prices do not form a clean comparison.” |
| 28–42s | Outcome key and evidence drawer | “Bell changes the route to `COMPARISON WITHHELD`. It shows the affected rows, identity path, timestamp and the contradiction.” |
| 42–54s | Next-action handoff | “The next step is explicit: resolve the unit, wrapper claim and quote identity, then verify issuer, redemption and execution.” |
| 54–68s | Search `Marvell`, open two factual rows | “When no critical Bell rule fires, the route is different. Marvell opens a facts-only comparison without declaring a winner.” |
| 68–80s | Decision brief and local worksheet | “The investor exports a brief containing the state, evidence, unresolved questions and next diligence step.” |
| 80–90s | Receipt JSON, freshness badge, GitHub and URL | “Every result is tied to a dated receipt. Bell is an integrity gate around CMC data, not a replacement for CMC or a buy recommendation.” |

## Non-negotiable proof frames

- Show the live `FRESH` badge and observed timestamp.
- Show the exact Silver state and the 31.05× observation.
- Open at least one evidence drawer so the video proves this is not a static
  slogan.
- Show Marvell as the contrasting `FACTS OPEN` route.
- Show the exported brief or worksheet.
- End on the public monitor, public GitHub and `/api/integrity` receipt.

## Claims the narrator must not make

- Bell does not prove backing, redemption, custody, eligibility, solvency or
  executable liquidity.
- `FACTS OPEN` is not approval and does not rank a wrapper.
- The public monitor does not expose the CMC API key or promise arbitrary live
  queries for every asset.
- The 31.05× figure is an observed surface contradiction in the published
  receipt, not proof of fraud or an investment loss.

## Hyperframe production notes

Use large typography and real interface crops. Keep the first frame to one
sentence and one visual question. Use a split-screen only for the CMC-versus-
Bell distinction; the evidence and next action should occupy the whole frame.
The final cut should let a judge understand the product without sound by
keeping the state labels and next action visible for at least two seconds.
