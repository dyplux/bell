Why the filter combines them

A reference can carry a published comparison and still have been flagged by the scan: it reads COMPARABLE · FLAGGED, and the flags are on its card under the verdict. The COMPARABLE filter gathers both, because a reader after the comparable set should not have to know there are two names for it. The export carries the distinction in its route column.

Use the index when you want to inspect the wider CMC RWA population

What the CSV containsExports exactly the references the filter and order above are showing, with the cheapest route, the observed spread in basis points and whether the cheapest route also has the highest reported 24h volume. Spread is an observed quote difference, not an executable saving.
