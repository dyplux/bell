      03 / EVIDENCE BEFORE INTERPRETATION

      A comparison you can defend

      01

      Join the CMC surfaces

      Start with the RWA grouping, then connect token, issuer, contract, market-pair and historical evidence. A single price call is not enough.

      02

      Run explicit integrity tests

      Flag unit or denomination signals, ticker collisions, derivatives mixed with spot, missing data and positive volume with zero market cap.

      03

      Ship the decision with limits

      Return provisionally comparable, investigate, do not compare or insufficient evidence. Every state carries the observations and unresolved questions behind it.

      What this snapshot can — and cannot — tell you +

      Observed

      Four wrappers, one seven-day window, three session buckets. Median hourly range and selected 24-hour venue totals from the workspace’s dated CMC research summary.

      Not measured

      The underlying Tesla stock price, bid–ask spreads, order-book depth, redemption rights, trade count or executable liquidity. A wide hour can be a single print.

      Clock limitation

      Bars crossing 09:30 ET are assigned by their opening timestamp. The source uses a weekday clock, not an exchange holiday calendar; weekday holidays remain in the cash bucket.

      The calculation

      hourly_range_pct = ((high - low) / open) × 100
session_range = median(hourly_range_pct)
relative_to_cash = session_range / cash_range × 100
cex_share = cex_24h / (cex_24h + dex_24h) × 100

      Relative figures are calculated from the rounded published medians. Venue shares are approximate because the source totals are rounded. No hourly volume is summed: the documented CMC hourly volume field is rolling 24-hour volume.
