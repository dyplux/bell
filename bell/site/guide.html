01 / THE PRODUCT

Bell is a pre-comparison integrity gate.

CoinMarketCap gives a user RWA references, token representations, issuers and quote fields. Bell joins those surfaces by stable identifiers and checks whether the rows can be compared without hiding denomination, identity or market-data contradictions.

What a reviewer can verify.

The numerical or field-level reason a rule fired.

Hashes for the input surfaces used by the run.

06 / HONEST LIMIT

Not a safety score.

The monitor does not prove backing, redemption, custody, legal eligibility, solvency or executable liquidity. `NO RULE HIT` means that this rule set found no contradiction. It does not mean approved.

The Startup plan does not expose the market-pairs evidence needed for a full venue comparison, so Bell records that boundary instead of inventing liquidity.
